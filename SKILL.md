# Generate Fresh Ideas

Turn any piece of content into 5 fresh derivative ideas — not copies, but
remixes. Each one uses a different creative lens to find a genuinely new angle
on the same core topic.

## When to Use

-   The user shares an article, tweet, or blog post and wants fresh content
    ideas from it
-   Someone says "remix this" or "give me new angles on this"
-   The user has notes or a transcript and wants to repurpose the material
-   Someone asks "what else can I make from this?" or "spin this into something
    new"
-   The user wants a contrarian take or a different perspective on existing
    content
-   Someone pastes a quote or excerpt and wants to riff on it
-   The user asks to "build on this" or find derivative ideas from source
    material

## Step 1: Accept the source material

Accept any input the user provides:

-   A URL to an article or post
-   Pasted text from any source
-   A quote or excerpt
-   A video/podcast transcript
-   The user's own notes or ideas

## Step 2: Apply the 5 remix lenses

For every piece of content, generate one idea through each of these lenses:

**1. 🔥 The Contrarian Take** What's the strongest counter-argument? Where is the
original wrong, incomplete, or missing nuance? Frame it as: "Everyone is saying
X, but here's why Y might actually be true." This isn't being disagreeable for
clicks — it's finding the genuine weakness in the argument.

**2. 📖 The Personal Story Hook** How could someone connect this idea to their
own lived experience? Find the personal angle: "This happened to me when..."
Frame it as a story with a lesson, not an abstract discussion. The idea should
be a springboard for a first-person narrative.

**3. 📊 The Data/Evidence Angle** What data, research, case study, or example
would make this idea more concrete? Suggest a version that leads with evidence
instead of opinion. "Here are the numbers behind this claim." If the original is
data-heavy, flip it — make it a human story instead.

**4. ✅ The Practical Playbook** Turn the idea into a step-by-step guide or
actionable checklist. Strip the theory and make it purely tactical: "How to
actually do [the thing the article talks about] in [timeframe]." The output
should be something someone can execute today.

**5. 🎯 The "What They Missed" Angle** What's the adjacent idea that the original
content didn't explore? The second-order consequence, the edge case, the related
insight that's potentially more interesting than the original. "The article
talks about X, but nobody's discussing Y, which matters more because..."

## Step 3: Format the output

Present the remixes in this structure:

```
## Remix: [Original Content Title/Topic]

🔥 CONTRARIAN: [One-line pitch]
   → [2-3 sentence expansion of the angle]

📖 PERSONAL STORY: [One-line pitch]
   → [2-3 sentence expansion + suggested story structure]

📊 DATA ANGLE: [One-line pitch]
   → [2-3 sentence expansion + suggested evidence to find]

✅ PLAYBOOK: [One-line pitch]
   → [2-3 sentence expansion + rough step outline]

🎯 WHAT THEY MISSED: [One-line pitch]
   → [2-3 sentence expansion of the adjacent idea]
```

## Step 4: Apply quality rules

-   Each remix must be different enough to stand on its own. If two feel too
    similar, cut one and go deeper on the other.
-   Don't be contrarian for the sake of it. The contrarian take should be
    genuinely defensible.
-   The personal story hook should be plausible for a range of people, not
    hyper-specific to one scenario.
-   The playbook must be actionable TODAY — not "step 1: get 10 years of
    experience."
-   If the original content is thin (a simple tweet or quote), some lenses may
    not apply. Skip them and note why: "The contrarian lens doesn't apply here —
    the take is already nuanced."
-   If the user asks to develop any of the 5 remixes into a full draft, do it
    immediately.

## Example

**"Remix this article about remote work burnout"** → Generate 5 angles: a
contrarian take ("remote work isn't the problem — bad management is"), a
personal story hook for connecting burnout to lived experience, a data/research
angle with burnout statistics, a practical anti-burnout playbook with actionable
steps, and a "what they missed" angle about hybrid being worse than full remote.
If the user likes one of the five, develop it into a full draft immediately.

## Gotchas

-   Remix quality depends on the depth of the source material — thin inputs
    produce thinner remixes
-   The contrarian lens may occasionally produce takes the user disagrees with —
    that's by design, not a bug
-   Cannot verify data suggestions — the "data angle" points the user toward
    evidence to find, not pre-verified statistics
-   Voice matching depends on the user having a voice profile; otherwise
    defaults to general conversational tone
